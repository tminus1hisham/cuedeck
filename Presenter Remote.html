Setup (keep this window on your laptop screen)
  1. Click Open deck window. You can also open DAMA Kenya Launch Event Deck.html yourself, but use the same browser as this remote.
  2. Drag the deck window onto the extended screen / projector.
  3. Click the full-screen button in the deck's top-right corner. That one click also lets the anthem and launch video play with sound.
  4. Come back here. Your keyboard, clicker or mouse on this window now controls the deck.
